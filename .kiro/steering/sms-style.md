# SMS style

Every outbound message must render within 160 GSM-7 characters for the sample data. Use plain words. One action per message. No price, customer note or phone number may appear in any message sent to a whole circle. Private messages (to the requester only) may include a price and pickup area but never another trader's phone number. Use credits for demo prices. Store amounts as integers in minor units. Use plain ASCII in outgoing text.

Good: "E7: 1 crate medium eggs, 4200 credits, Cedar Lane side, hold till 6pm. Reply 1."
Bad: "Ellis has eggs at 4,200 credits. Her number is included."
